# Privacy Policy – Offline Scan PDF

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Offline Scan PDF ("the app") is published by v98881. This policy explains how
the app handles data.

## Summary

The app does **not** collect, store on servers, transmit or share any personal
data. Everything happens on your device.

## Processing on your device

- **Camera:** The camera is used only while you scan pages. Photos are taken
  only when you press the capture button or have enabled automatic capture.
- **Documents:** Photos, processed pages and PDFs are stored only in the app's
  private storage on your device. They are excluded from cloud backups made by
  the app platform and are deleted when you uninstall the app or delete the
  documents in the app.
- **Settings:** Language, theme and scan settings are stored locally on your
  device.

## No network access

The app works completely offline. The Android version does not request the
internet permission. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or
crash reporting services, and it has no user accounts.

## Sharing

Files leave the app only when you share or export them yourself using the
system share dialog. The recipient app or service you choose is then
responsible for that data under its own privacy policy.

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Camera | Scanning document pages |

## Children

The app does not collect any data, including from children.

## Your rights

Because the publisher receives no data from you, there is no data about you
that could be accessed, corrected or deleted at the publisher. You control all
documents in the app and can delete them at any time.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an app update
and on the app's support page.
